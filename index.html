あそび方

Desktop. Arrow keys or WASD to walk. Shift hurries. Click the map or a pin to stroll there. Enter does the thing in the speech bubble.

Phone. The pad walks. Tap the map to set a destination. はや足 is a faster step. Pinch to zoom.

日常 are ordinary Tokyo errands: an onigiri, the scramble, a station stamp, ramen, a vending machine, Yoyogi Park, Meiji Jingu, Tokyo Tower.

ひみつ are neighborhood things, marked with a gold badge. Each one ends with a short local tip. Read it again from the quest list.

電車 drops you at a real station so Gotokuji, Yanaka, or Kichijoji are a ride plus a short walk, not an hour of holding an arrow.

Each quest opens a cartoon of that real place, with you in the picture. The location card names the real spot, and 📓 keeps the stamp, your omamori, and your capsules.

Some alleys open after dark and some markets only in the daytime. The clock moves when you finish a quest. ¥500 on the train board opens Asakusa, Ueno, Akihabara, and Ginza.

You can cross blocks freely. The map underneath is real; the tiles are softened so the day feels like a picture book.

The map is drawn from OpenStreetMap roads, parks, water, and buildings, in a pastel cartoon style. Tiles © OpenStreetMap contributors, © OpenMapTiles, OpenFreeMap. Places were read on 2026-11-17. Data © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.
